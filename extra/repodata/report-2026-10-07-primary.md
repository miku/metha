## Deduplication and aggregators

This section always covers all endpoints. Records are matched across endpoints by a hash of
OAI identifier and normalized title. Each endpoint gets one role:

* **variant**: same repository as a larger endpoint, because the URLs only differ in scheme,
  case, `www.`, default port or trailing slash, or because ≥ 90% of its records are shared and
  the sizes are within 20% of each other
* **subset**: ≥ 90% of its records are contained in a larger endpoint (e.g. a HAL portal, or a
  single journal also served by a site-wide OJS endpoint); or ≥ 50% of its titles occur in a
  *union endpoint* at the same registered domain
* **aggregator**: on a curated list of aggregation services, or detected because it contains
  at least half of the titles of ≥ 10 other repositories (with ≥ 20 titles each), mostly at
  other registered domains, and ≥ 40% of its own titles occur elsewhere; if the contained
  repositories are mostly at its own domain, it is a union endpoint instead
* **primary**: everything else; the canonical endpoint of a repository unit

The deduplicated scope (`-scope primary`) merges each unit's endpoints (the primary one plus
its variants and subsets) and keeps every record once: by record key, latest version first, and
dropping records of other members whose title also occurs at the canonical endpoint. Platforms
on a curated list of national union repositories (HAL) are never flagged as aggregators.
Records shared by more than 50 endpoints are ignored for matching.
