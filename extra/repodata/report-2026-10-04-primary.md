## Deduplication and aggregators

This section always covers all endpoints. Records are matched across endpoints by a hash of
OAI identifier and normalized title. Each endpoint gets one role:

* **variant**: same repository as a larger endpoint, because the URLs only differ in scheme,
  case, `www.`, default port or trailing slash, or because ≥ 90% of its records are shared and
  the sizes are within 20% of each other
* **subset**: ≥ 90% of its records are contained in a larger endpoint (e.g. a HAL portal, or a
  single journal also served by a site-wide OJS endpoint); or ≥ 80% of its titles occur in a
  *union endpoint* at the same registered domain
* **aggregator**: on a curated list of aggregation services, or detected because it contains
  at least half of the titles of ≥ 10 other repositories (with ≥ 20 titles each), mostly at
  other registered domains; if they are mostly at its own domain, it is a union endpoint instead
* **primary**: everything else

The deduplicated scope (`-scope primary`) keeps primary endpoints and, within each, only the
latest version of every record. Records shared by more than 50 endpoints are ignored for matching.
